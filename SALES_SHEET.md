## Key features

**📝 Questions & Quizzes**
- Organised library: streams → subjects → topics → sessions → quizzes
- Many question types: MCQ, **Numerical MCQ**, matching, statement-based, pair, pair-select, assertion & reason, table, journal entry, ledger posting, sentence rearrangement
- Instant results with detailed, per-option explanations

**🖥 Online CBT Exams**
- Real computer-based tests with scheduling & entry windows
- Email-OTP student registration, automatic ranking, emailed results, live control

**🤖 AI Question Generator**
- Generate questions by topic or **per subtopic** with live progress
- Generate from a **pasted source, file, web page or YouTube link**
- **Auto-research the web** for current-affairs topics
- Descriptive + numerical questions, with a built-in question checker

**📚 Study Material, Test Series & Papers**
- Test series, previous-year papers, study material (institution → subject → class → files)
- PDF builder, handwritten notes, downloadable resources

**👨‍🎓 Students, Clients & Analytics**
- Student & paying-client accounts, subscriptions with online payments
- Dashboards, leaderboards, topic-wise performance tracking
- Backup & restore of content (with live progress)

**🎨 White-label & Engagement**
- Full branding from the admin panel: name, logo, colours, font
- Notice board, feedback, messaging, and optional Facebook/Instagram auto-posting
- Installable mobile app experience (PWA); content protection (watermark, copy/screenshot guard)
